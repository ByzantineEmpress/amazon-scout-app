**Books with no barcode can be scanned now.** Older and collectable books often have an ISBN
printed but no barcode — or predate ISBN entirely. Tap **📖 Scan Printed ISBN** in the bottom bar
and either read the number off the page or search by title. No more typing in a hurry.

### ✨ New

- **Scan a printed ISBN.** Point the camera at the number — 10 or 13 digits, with or without
  hyphens — and it locks on in about a second. Every number is checksum-verified, so a misread is
  dropped rather than looked up: if the print is poor it stays quiet instead of sending you to the
  wrong listing.
- **Look up pre-1970 books by title.** Books older than ISBN have no number printed at all, so the
  **Title** tab reads the title page, lets you correct the details, and searches OpenLibrary —
  whose coverage of old and out-of-print titles is far better than a retail catalogue. Pick the
  right edition and it looks up exactly as a scan would.
- **🔦 Light button** for faded print on an old copyright page.

### 📝 Worth knowing

- A pre-1970 book will not be found by the ISBN tab — there is no number on it to read. Use the
  Title tab for those.
- The Title tab guesses from typography, so it is sometimes wrong. Both fields are editable, and
  you choose from the matches rather than the app choosing for you.

### ✅ Unchanged

- Barcode scanning, prices, restriction warnings and the in-app updater all work exactly as before.
