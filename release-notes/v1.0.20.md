**One fix on top of v1.0.19.** A photo whose only readable text was compliance marks — EN71,
ASTM, RoHS — produced a nonsense search for those marks. It now reports that nothing could be
read and points you at the label, which is what the screen is supposed to do.

### 🐛 Fixed

- **Compliance marks are no longer searched for.** They are neither a product name nor a model
  number, so a bare plastic item marked only with standards now sends you to the label instead of
  searching for "EN71 ASTM F963 RoHS" and finding nothing.

### ✅ Unchanged

- Everything from v1.0.19: model numbers read from a sticker, barcode reading, and the sold comps
  pane. Barcode scanning, Amazon prices, book comps and in-app updates are all untouched.
