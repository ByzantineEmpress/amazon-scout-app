**Books with an "X" in the ISBN are fine — and always were.** Nothing about how they scan changed.
The letter is never part of the barcode; it is produced by the app after scanning. This release
adds a safety net for a rare barcode layout, plus tests that lock the behaviour in.

### ✅ Unchanged

- Scanning, lookup speed, price sources and the restriction rules are all untouched.
- **⌨️ Type Barcode / ISBN** still accepts a printed ISBN-10 that ends in X, exactly as before.

### 📝 What is in this build

- **A book barcode reported together with its small price add-on is now read correctly.** Books
  often carry a 2- or 5-digit price supplement next to the main barcode. If your phone ever
  reports the two as one long number, the book would previously have been skipped without any
  explanation.
- **The ISBN conversion that picks which Amazon listing to look up is now covered by tests** —
  including the X case (9780439420891 resolves to the ASIN 043942089X).

### 🔎 Why the letter is never scanned

A book's barcode is always an EAN-13: thirteen digits, prefix 978 or 979. The ISBN-10 — the form
that can end in X — is printed as plain text and derived by the app from those thirteen digits.
So an X can never reach the scanner, and the stricter scanning in the previous release cannot
affect books.
