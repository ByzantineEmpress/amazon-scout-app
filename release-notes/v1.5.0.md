**The comps view and the type-in dialog are rebuilt.** Two more screens on the new design — and the
comps view, where sell-through and the category live, now reads like the rest of the app.

### ✨ New

- **Comps view rebuilt.** Same header and status language as everywhere else, with the sell-through
  badge and the category chip where you expect them, and eBay's listings below. The page-reading
  logic underneath is unchanged — it is the reason the numbers appear at all.
- **Type-in dialog rebuilt.** Paste or type; hyphens and spaces are stripped for you, and Cancel /
  Look up are where your thumb expects them.
- **The category still applies itself** as soon as eBay's page says which category the search
  belongs to, with one tap to search everything.

### 📝 Worth knowing

- **One screen left on the old design: the printed-ISBN tool.** Once that is rebuilt, the new
  interface becomes the default and the old one is deleted.
- Still behind **Settings → Interface → "New interface"** until then.

### ✅ Unchanged

- Scanning, prices, restriction warnings, sell-through, and in-app updates.
