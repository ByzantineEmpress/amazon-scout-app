**Book comps now search eBay by title.** eBay sellers type a book's title and author and almost
never its ISBN — so searching by ISBN found nothing, and books looked like they had no sold comps
at all. The card now searches the title plus the author, and shows you exactly what it searched.

### ✨ New

- **Sold comps by title.** The search terms are the book's main title plus the author's surname,
  which is how listings are actually written. Subtitles, series numbers and "[DVD]" style asides
  are dropped — eBay requires every word you type to appear in a listing, so an over-long search
  quietly returns nothing.
- **You can see the search terms**, and switch between **Title** and **ISBN** with one tap. The
  ISBN is still there for the listings that do quote a number, and for non-book items.
- **The instant price tries the title first**, then falls back to the ISBN, so you get a figure
  far more often. The card now says which one it matched on rather than calling every result
  "verified" — a title match is a good indicator, not a guarantee of the same edition.

### ✅ Unchanged

- Barcode scanning, Amazon prices, restriction warnings, and in-app updates.
