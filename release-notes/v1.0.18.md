**New: photograph any item and look up its eBay sold comps.** Tap **📷 Photo Comps** on the main
screen, frame the thing, and take one picture. It works for anything that is not a book — toys,
tools, games, kitchenware, records, vintage anything — where there is no barcode or catalogue
entry to lean on.

### ✨ New

- **📷 Photo Comps**, a third button beside Printed ISBN and Type In.
- **One picture identifies the item two ways at once.** It reads the text on the packaging, and
  it also tries to decode a barcode from the photo — if there is one, the item is named exactly
  from the catalogue; if not, the packaging text carries the search. The better answer wins.
- **A lowest sold figure**, plus the full sold & completed listings in the same in-app pane the
  scan card uses.
- **Everything stays editable.** The search terms land in a field you can fix before searching,
  and if a barcode was read you can tap to search that instead.

### 📝 Worth knowing

- **Nothing here is written to History.** This is a lookup, not a scan, so it will not clutter
  your scan log with things you were only pricing.
- Reading a barcode out of a photo is an Android advantage; on iOS this falls back to the
  packaging text.
- It reads the largest text on the packaging, which is usually but not always the product name —
  that is why the field is editable before anything is searched.

### ✅ Unchanged

- Barcode scanning, Amazon prices, book comps, restriction warnings, in-app updates.
