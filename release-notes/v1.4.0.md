**The photo comps screen is rebuilt.** Photographing an item now looks and behaves like the rest of
the new interface — same type, same buttons, same spacing — instead of being the one screen that
belonged to the old app.

### ✨ New

- **Comps tab rebuilt:** the camera full-bleed, a panel that asks one thing at a time, the editable
  search field, and the model / barcode suggestions as chips you can tap.
- **All three ways in now behave alike:** a barcode and a printed ISBN end on the item screen, and a
  photograph ends on the comps screen — each in the new design, with nothing presented a third way.
- **Lowest sold and sell-through on the screen itself**, with the full listings a tap away.
- **Nothing readable is not a dead end:** it says so, puts the cursor in the search field, and
  suggests trying the label underneath.

### 📝 Worth knowing

- Still behind **Settings → Interface → "New interface"**.
- The comps pane and the two small tools (type-in, printed ISBN) are still the old components. They
  work and they are reachable — they are just the last screens still wearing the old styles.
- The classic interface is untouched, so this changes nothing until you switch.

### ✅ Unchanged

- Scanning, prices, restriction warnings, sell-through, and in-app updates.
