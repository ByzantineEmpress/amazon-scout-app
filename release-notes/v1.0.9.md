**eBay sold comps now open inside the app.** Tap **⚡ View Sold Comps** on any scan and the sold
& completed listings appear right in Scout — filtered to sold items, cheapest first. Your scan
stays behind them, so **Done** puts you straight back on the item.

No more bouncing out to the eBay app just to price a book or DVD.

### ✨ New

- **In-app eBay sold comps.** Real sold and completed listings in a full-screen pane — nothing to
  leave, no scan to lose.
- **In-app browser fallback.** If eBay won't load in the embedded view, tap **Browser** and the
  same page opens in an in-app tab. You still never leave Amazon Scout.
- **Back navigation.** Swipe back on iOS, or tap **‹**, to move around eBay's results.

### 📝 Unchanged

- The instant *sold lowest* figure on the scan card works exactly as before.
- **Open eBay ↗** is still there for when you'd rather hand off to the eBay app.
