**Fixed a crash when scanning.** v1.0.9 could close the app the moment a scan result appeared, or
when opening a past scan from History. If you are on v1.0.9, update as soon as you can — sorry
about that one.

### 🐛 Fixed

- Scanning a barcode no longer crashes the app.
- Opening a past scan from History no longer crashes the app.

### ✨ Also in this update

- **eBay sold comps now open inside the app.** Tap **⚡ View Sold Comps** on any scan to see real
  sold and completed listings without leaving Scout — filtered to sold items, cheapest first.
  Your scan stays behind them, so **Done** puts you straight back on the item. If eBay won't load
  in the embedded view, tap **Browser** for an in-app tab instead.

### ⚠️ Coming from v1.0.7 or earlier?

Those builds were signed with a different key, so Android cannot install this over them.
Uninstall Amazon Scout once, then install this APK — in-app updates work normally from then on.
