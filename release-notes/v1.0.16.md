**Fixed the black bars on the "Scan Printed ISBN" screen.** The camera preview now fills the
screen instead of sitting in a strip at the top with black underneath it.

### 🐛 Fixed

- **The preview fills the frame.** The camera sat in a fixed-height box and the video refused to
  fill it, so the image occupied the top of the box and the rest stayed black. The preview is now
  full-screen with the controls floating over it — the same arrangement the main scanner uses.
- **A much bigger viewfinder**, so faded or small print is easier for the reader to make out.

### 📝 Worth knowing

- The controls moved slightly: **🔦 Light** and **Done** are in the header, the tabs sit under
  them, and the status and search results now live in a panel at the bottom. That panel scrolls
  when a title search returns a lot of matches.

### ✅ Unchanged

- Reading numbers is silent, checksum-verified, and needs two agreeing frames before it offers a
  number. Barcode scanning, prices and in-app updates are all unchanged.
