**The scanner only trusts real product barcodes now.** A shelf QR code, a price tag or a shipping
label can no longer be mistaken for your item, and a single blurry frame no longer sends the app
off looking up the wrong number. Hold the barcode in the frame a split second longer and it locks
on.

### 🐛 Fixed

- **Barcodes that are not products are ignored.** QR codes, asset tags and shipping labels were
  having their digits extracted and looked up as if they were your item — that is where the
  nonsense results came from.
- **A one-frame misread no longer triggers a lookup.** The same code has to be seen twice in a
  row, which takes well under a tenth of a second.
- **Codes with an invalid check digit are rejected**, so a smudged or partly covered barcode is
  passed over instead of guessed at.

### ✨ New

- **"Hold steady to confirm..."** appears under the reticle when a code has been seen and is being
  verified, so you can tell the difference between "checking that" and "nothing happened".

### 📝 Notes

- Scanning covers **EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A and ITF-14** — the formats actually used on books, DVDs,
  Blu-rays and games.
- Small-package **UPC-E** barcodes (gum, lip balm and the like) are no longer scanned, because
  they cannot be validated reliably yet. Use **⌨️ Type Barcode / ISBN** for those.
- Nothing about lookup speed or the price sources changed.
