**One result screen for everything.** A barcode, a printed ISBN, or an old scan tapped in History
now open the same screen, in the new design — instead of a 917-line modal that had its own layout,
its own styles and its own idea of what to show.

### ✨ New

- **A single item screen** shared by the scanner, the ISBN tool and History: the verdict and what it
  is based on, Amazon's prices, the eBay comps search, the identifiers, and the actions that open
  Amazon or Seller Central.
- **Comps open from there**, in the view where eBay's page is actually loaded — which is where
  sell-through and the sold counts really live.
- **The verdict now says what it is:** a local guess from the publisher and ISBN, with a line
  pointing at Seller Central. It no longer reads like an answer from Amazon.

### 📝 Worth knowing

- **The scraped eBay price is gone from the result.** eBay refuses the app's requests (HTTP 403), so
  that figure was usually missing and occasionally stale. One source of truth — the comps view — is
  better than a number that sometimes appears.
- Still behind **Settings → Interface → "New interface"**; the classic interface is unchanged.
- The photo-comps screen is the last one still on the old design.

### ✅ Unchanged

- Scanning, prices, restriction warnings, sell-through, and in-app updates.
